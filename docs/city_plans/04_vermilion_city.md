# Vermilion City — "The Garrison"
## Narrative Plan (Phase 2: Complication)

---

## 1. Overview

**Tone:** Tense, militaristic, oppressive. This is the first city where the League's authority feels like a weight rather than a shield. Vermilion is a port city — rougher, busier, more diverse than Cerulean or Pewter — but the visible League military presence under Lt. Surge's command transforms what should feel like freedom into surveillance.

**Narrative Role:** Vermilion is the player's entry into Phase 2 (Complication). The clean good-vs-evil framing of Phase 1 starts to crack. Surge represents the authoritarian pole of the fusion debate: fusion is a weapon, the League is the authority, dissent is treason. City NPCs are split between those who feel protected and those who feel controlled. The player is asked a direct flavor question — do you agree with Surge? — and their answer is recorded for downstream dialogue callbacks.

**Thematic Core:** Security vs. freedom. Who gets to decide what "safe" means?

## 3. Dialogue Direction

### A. Lt. Surge & Gym Trainers

**Surge's Voice:** Blunt, military cadence. Short sentences. No hedging. He speaks like a man who has seen combat and believes the only thing between civilization and chaos is force. He respects strength and despises weakness. He is not cruel — he is certain. That certainty is what makes him dangerous.

**Pre-Battle (Surge):**
- Opens with sizing the player up. Not hostile, but not warm. A commanding officer assessing a recruit.
- Delivers his thesis directly: fusion is a weapon. Weapons belong in the hands of people trained to use them. That means the League. End of discussion.
- References real threats — Team Rocket, foreign regions, wild Pokemon surges — to justify the garrison. He is not wrong about the threats. He is wrong about the solution being the only possible one.
- Mentions that he has fused Pokemon in his own team and treats it as standard military procedure — you use every advantage available.
- Challenge line should feel like an order, not an invitation.

**Post-Battle (Surge):**
- Respects the player's strength. "You earned it. That's all that matters to me."
- Gives Thunder Badge + TM73 Thunder Wave. **Preserve all mechanical rewards.**
- Then the choice moment (see Section 4).

**Gym Trainers (Events 4, 6, 7):**
- Frame as soldiers/veterans under Surge's command.
- Event 4: Young recruit. Eager. Believes in the mission. Post-defeat: gives puzzle hint (preserve).
- Event 6: Seasoned. "This is no place for kids" becomes "This gym is a military installation, not a playground." Post-defeat: gives lock hint (preserve).
- Event 7: Veteran. "When I was in the Army, Lt. Surge was my strict CO" — expand this. Talk about how Surge's Pokemon saved lives. Post-defeat: respect for the player. Preserve puzzle hint.

**Gym Guide (Event 11):**
- Preserve type matchup info and puzzle hint.
- Reframe "Yo! Champ in making!" — could keep this or adjust to something that acknowledges the military atmosphere.

### B. City NPCs — Divided Opinions

The city should feel split. Some NPCs appreciate Surge's security. Others chafe under it. Neither side is presented as wholly right.

**Pro-Security NPCs (new or rewritten):**
- A sailor who defends Surge: "You want Rockets running the docks? Because that's what happens without security." (From story bible.)
- A parent who feels their children are safer: "I sleep better knowing Surge's patrols are out there."
- The construction worker (Event 19) could mention the new structures are League-funded security infrastructure.

**Anti-Security NPCs (new or rewritten):**
- A shopkeeper who complains that Surge's checkpoints are hurting trade: "Used to be this port was open to everyone. Now every ship gets searched. Business is down 40%." (From story bible.)
- A young person who feels watched: "Every corner has a camera or a Magneton patrol. They say it's for our safety, but whose safety?"
- An older NPC who remembers Vermilion before Surge: "This city used to be free. You could feel the ocean breeze without a uniform blocking your view."

**Neutral/Ambivalent NPCs:**

**Setup Line (Surge):** "One more thing, kid. You're strong. That means you've got a say in how this works. So I'm asking: the League runs fusion. The League keeps order. You agree with that?"
