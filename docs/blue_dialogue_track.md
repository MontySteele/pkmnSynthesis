# Blue (Rival) — Complete Dialogue Track

All dialogue uses RPG Maker XP text conventions:
- `\PN` = Player name
- `\V[N]` = Variable value
- `\C[2]text\C[0]` = Color highlight (red)
- Lines are kept under ~45 characters for the text box width.

## Encounter 1: Pallet Town — Oak's Lab

**Context:** Shared (pre-fork). The player and Blue meet at Oak's Lab. Oak gives them starter Pokémon. Blue is excited and warm — this is their best friend and this is the adventure they've dreamed about.

### Before receiving starters

**Blue:**
> \PN! You're finally here!

> I've been pacing around the lab for like
> an hour. Professor Oak said today's the day!

> Can you believe it? We're actually going to
> be trainers. Real, licensed trainers.

> I've been dreaming about this since we were
> kids. Remember when we used to pretend the
> tall grass was a Route and your mom's
> Meowth was a wild Pokémon?

> ...She was not happy about that.

### After Oak's speech and starter selection

**Blue:**
> Nice choice! I'll take this one, then.

> Hey — before we head out, let's battle.
> Right here, right now. Our first real fight
> as real trainers!

> Don't go easy on me, \PN.
> I won't go easy on you.

### After battle (Blue wins)

**Blue:**
> Ha! First battle, first win! Don't worry —
> you'll catch up. You always do.

> Alright, I'm heading out. Route 1's
> waiting. I'll see you in Pewter, yeah?

> This is going to be amazing. I can feel it.

### After battle (Blue loses)

**Blue:**
> ...Okay, wow. You're actually good at this.

> I mean, I knew you would be. That's why
> this is going to be fun — I need someone
> worth chasing.

> Alright, I'm heading out. Route 1's
> waiting. I'll see you in Pewter, yeah?

## Voice Notes

**Blue's arc in one sentence:** A person who starts loving the world and ends loving a person they're afraid of.

**Key vocal shifts:**
- **Pallet → Cerulean:** Warm, excited, breezy. Uses "!" often. Asks questions for fun.
- **Mt. Moon → Lavender:** Quieter. Questions become real, not rhetorical. Starts trailing off mid-thought.
- **Silph Co.:** Urgent, decisive. This is where Blue becomes an adult.
- **Post-fork (both paths):** Measured, careful. Every word costs something.
- **Victory Road → Champion:** Tender and devastating. Blue never raises their voice. The calm is what hurts.

**Pronouns:** Blue uses "they/them" to stay gender-neutral for player customization. All dialogue should work regardless of how the player imagines Blue.

**Line length:** Keep dialogue under ~45 characters per line for the RPG Maker text box. Break on natural speech pauses, not arbitrary character limits.
